IPM

Our approach

Smarter pest management, with safety at the centre

Flatline uses the internationally recognized Integrated Pest Management (IPM) approach. IPM looks beyond a single treatment by considering the pest, the property, contributing conditions and appropriate control methods together.

This allows the team to recommend a tailored plan while upholding strong safety standards and internationally recognized good practice.

  • Inspect and identify the problem
  • Consider the property and contributing conditions
  • Choose an appropriate, targeted response
  • Monitor results and provide follow-up where required
