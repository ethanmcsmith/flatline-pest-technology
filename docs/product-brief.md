# Flatline Pest Technology — Product Brief

## Product vision

Create a fast, trustworthy digital front door for Flatline Pest Technology that helps people across Trinidad and Tobago understand their pest problem, choose the right service, and request an inspection with minimal effort.

The website should function as both a lead-generation product for the business and a measurable content-design case study.

## Business goals

1. Generate qualified enquiries through WhatsApp, phone calls, and quote requests.
2. Prioritize high-value termite work without hiding the full service range.
3. Establish Flatline as a responsive, professional, and personally attentive provider.
4. improve local-search visibility for specific pest problems and services.
5. Create a baseline for measuring and improving the customer journey.

## Primary conversion

Request an inspection and tailored quotation.

## Supporting conversions

- Start a WhatsApp conversation.
- Call Flatline.
- Explore a relevant service.
- Confirm that Flatline serves the customer's property type and location.

## Audiences

### Homeowners and tenants

People who need a fast, reassuring answer to an active pest problem or want preventative treatment.

### Property and business decision-makers

Property managers, facilities teams, business owners, contractors, and developers who need dependable service, documentation, tailored treatment, and follow-up.

### Marine customers

Owners or operators of yachts, sailboats, and other vessels who need pest treatment in a specialist environment.

### Construction customers

Homeowners, contractors, and developers looking for pre-construction termite protection.

## Customer jobs

- Identify whether Flatline treats the pest or problem.
- Know whether service is available in their area and property type.
- Understand what happens after making contact.
- Get a prompt inspection without committing to an unknown price.
- Feel safe inviting a technician onto the property.
- Understand whether follow-up support or a warranty is available.

## Core proposition

Fast, personalized pest control across Trinidad and Tobago, beginning with an inspection and a treatment plan tailored to the property.

## Differentiators

- Same-day inspections may be available when scheduling permits.
- Inspections are otherwise commonly completed the next day.
- Quotations and treatments are tailored to the customer and property.
- The business reports that technicians complete two to three technical training workshops per year.
- A supplied certificate documents technical training in pest control, including flea and tick, termite, and bird control, completed on 23 June 2026.
- The business carries workers' compensation and public liability insurance.
- Follow-up support is available across services.
- Treatment-dependent warranties may range from one to ten years.
- Residential, commercial, construction, lawn, sanitation, and marine environments are served.

All timing and warranty language must remain qualified. The website must not imply that same-day service or a particular warranty is guaranteed before an assessment.

## Availability

Appointments can be arranged for any day and time. Calls are welcome from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.; after hours, WhatsApp is preferred for routine requests. Same-day appointments may be available when scheduling permits. Emergency calls are accepted, and emergency-service fees may apply and should be confirmed before service is arranged.

## Pricing strategy

Do not publish fixed treatment prices. Explain that pest type, property, infestation, treatment method, and follow-up needs affect cost. Inspections are free in Trinidad. Inspections in Tobago or any other Caribbean island or country carry a fee to cover travel costs, confirmed before booking. Use the inspection request and tailored quote as the low-friction next step.
