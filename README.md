# Flatline Pest Technology

Conversion-focused website and product case study for a pest-control company serving Trinidad and Tobago.

## Project goals

- Generate qualified calls, WhatsApp conversations, and quote requests.
- Build trust before customers invite a technician onto their property.
- Make services and service areas easy to understand on mobile.
- Establish a measurable local-search and conversion baseline.
- Document product and content-design decisions for a portfolio case study.
