Good to know

Common questions

Quick answers before you book.

Are inspections and quotations free?

Quotations are free. Inspections are free for properties in Trinidad. Inspections in Tobago or any other Caribbean island or country carry a fee to cover travel costs, which Flatline will confirm before the appointment is booked.

How much will treatment cost?

Pricing is assessed case by case. The pest, property, activity level, treatment method and follow-up needs all affect the final quotation.

Do you provide warranties?

Eligible services may include warranties from one to ten years. The available term depends on the treatment, project and agreed scope.

Do you work outside normal business hours?

Yes. Appointments can be arranged for any day and time, and same-day appointments may be available. Calls are welcome from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.; after hours, WhatsApp is preferred for routine requests. Emergency calls are accepted, and emergency-service fees may apply. Any applicable fee will be discussed before service is arranged.

Do you cover Tobago and other Caribbean islands?

Flatline serves all of Trinidad and Tobago. Selected work elsewhere in the Caribbean may be available by arrangement.
